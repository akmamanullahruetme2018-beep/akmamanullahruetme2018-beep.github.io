01 / FLAGSHIP RESEARCHPEER-REVIEWED · PUBLISHED 2026

Integrated hydrogen potential
from municipal organic waste.

JOURNAL ARTICLE

A.K.M. Amanullah and Md. Rabiul Islam Sarker
International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, Volume 254 (2026), Article 156339.

Open DOI: 10.1016/j.ijhydene.2026.156339 ↗

The question

How much hydrogen might be recovered from Bangladesh's municipal organic waste if waste supply forecasts, process constraints and economic uncertainty are modeled together rather than independently?

The approach

Combine 22 years of waste data from six city corporations with ARIMA–XGBoost forecasting; simulate anaerobic digestion–steam methane reforming in Aspen Plus V12; evaluate exergy, life-cycle impacts, costs, and uncertainty using Monte Carlo and Sobol sensitivity analysis.

What the evidence means

The numbers above are reported forecasting, process-simulation and scenario estimates, not demonstrated commercial-plant production. My next research interest is strengthening uncertainty propagation and validation with measured plant or pilot-scale data.

02 / THERMAL ENGINEERINGJOURNAL MANUSCRIPT IN PREPARATION

Thermoelectric-assisted
evaporative cooling.

EXPERIMENT + SIMULATION

With Md. Emdadul Hoque and collaborators. Research on compact, direct evaporative cooling assisted by thermoelectric pre-cooling in humid tropical conditions.

Hardware concept

Four TEC1-12706 modules pre-cool recirculating water delivered to honeycomb evaporative pads, while a secondary liquid loop removes heat from the module hot sides.

Statistical analysis

Twelve source-reported prototype comparisons at 55–83% relative humidity were analyzed separately from a 29-run, four-factor Box–Behnken simulation dataset. Nine fitted quadratic response surfaces were reproduced; R² values were reported from 0.9904 to 0.9997 for local simulation fitting.

Interpretation, not overclaiming

The mean paired air-temperature reduction advantage was 2.63 °C. High simulation R² does not establish independent experimental validation, full-system COP or room-level comfort performance. The manuscript explicitly identifies unresolved heat-balance and humidity-measurement requirements.

03 / BIOENERGYICMIME 2026 · PAPER 318 · ACCEPTED¹

Long-term anaerobic
co-digestion of cafeteria waste.

Minhazul Alom, A.K.M. Amanullah (corresponding author), and collaborators. Eight feedstock configurations at 37 ± 1 °C, observed for unequal periods of 20–130 days.

Study objective

Understand methane-concentration and pH trends among combinations of vegetable peelings, rice, cow manure and urea during mesophilic anaerobic digestion.

Selected observations

Papaya and bottle gourd mixed (S6) reached 66.96% methane fraction at day 130; papaya alone had an earlier peak of 80.75% at day 75, followed by decline. The cross-scenario pH–CH₄ correlation was weak and not statistically significant (r = 0.373, p = 0.363).

Measurement boundary

These are gas-composition fractions, not methane yields. Gas volume was not measured and reactors were unreplicated, so no comparative methane production efficiency or causal inhibition mechanism is claimed.

04 / WATER–ENERGY NEXUSCONFERENCE MANUSCRIPT · DECISION UNCONFIRMED

Flow-electrode
capacitive deionization.

Co-author on a modeling study exploring salt-removal performance, electrical energy demand, and multi-objective Pareto optimization for flow-electrode capacitive deionization.

Method

Physics-informed semi-empirical modeling of salt-removal efficiency versus applied voltage, flow rate and electrode carbon loading; compare model trends with published experimental literature.

Result & boundary

Selected simulated conditions produced predicted removal efficiency above 90% near 1.1–1.3 V, with higher voltages increasing modeled energy use. These are model predictions; independently replicated new laboratory observations were not provided.

Publication status

A full ICMIE conference manuscript was provided. This website does not imply that the work has already been accepted, published or assigned a final DOI.

Industrial processes,
smaller environmental costs.

Three collaborative manuscripts on textile finishing, performance and resource use. All four ICMIME papers on this site are described as accepted for the forthcoming conference, not already published in proceedings.

PAPER 331

Comparative Assessment of Conventional Bleach and Ozone-Assisted Denim Washing: Resource Use, Fabric Performance, and Environmental Impact

Recipe water use decreased from 360 L to 205 L in the ozone routes (43.1%), but mechanical properties varied substantially, requiring process-specific trade-off assessment.

Naeem Ahmmed, A.K.M. Amanullah, and collaborators · Accepted¹
PAPER 338

Comparative Assessment of Conventional and Alternative Stripping Processes for Reactive-Dyed Cotton Fabrics

Compared hydrosulfite–NaOH and NaCl–NaOH stripping across fabric performance and wastewater indicators. Alternative treatment showed lower recorded BOD/COD, subject to salinity and replication caveats.

Eshita Datta, A.K.M. Amanullah (co-corresponding), and collaborators · Accepted¹
PAPER 339

Development and Preliminary Evaluation of a Bio-Based Multifunctional Finish for Cotton: Antibacterial Activity, Flame-Spread Behavior and Fabric Performance

Reported 99.27% bacterial reduction against Staphylococcus aureus in an independent test. Flame-retardant performance was not established because of inconsistent source flame-spread records.

Naeem Ahmmed, A.K.M. Amanullah, and collaborators · Accepted¹
